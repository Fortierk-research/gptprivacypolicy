# gptprivacypolicy
Privacy policy for GPTs

Last updated: June 29, 2026
This GPT is designed to support users by providing educational and informational assistance.
Information Collected:  This GPT does not independently collect, store, or sell personal information.   
Conversations may be processed by OpenAI according to OpenAI’s privacy practices.
User-Provided Information:  
Users should avoid entering sensitive personal, health, financial, student, or confidential information 
unless they are comfortable sharing it in ChatGPT.

Use of Information:  Information entered by users is used only to generate responses during the interaction except for initial_PEAT_score, exam_date,	days_until_exam,	weak_content_areas,	session_goal,	prompt_category,	engagement_level, and	follow_up_count.  It will not be tied to any user specific identifiable information.
